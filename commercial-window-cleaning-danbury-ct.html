Commercial Window Cleaning in Danbury

Danbury's commercial property is spread along I-84: corporate and office parks on the west side, medical buildings around the hospital, and big retail along Newtown Road, Federal Road and the area around the mall, plus older storefronts and offices downtown on Main Street.

Highway-side retail and offices pick up road film and exhaust faster than buildings on quiet streets, so storefronts near the interstate usually look best on a quarterly exterior schedule. We clean them early or after close so it never competes with customers.

Office parks and medical buildings here are mostly low and mid rise, which is exactly what our purified water fed pole system is built for. It reaches up to about four stories from the ground, with no lifts in the parking lot and no ladders against the building. Pure water dries spot free with no soap residue.

Facilities managers who need a paper trail usually go with our Executive program: interior and exterior glass twice a year and a photo condition report after every visit. Multi building properties can be put on one account with one point of contact.

We run Fairfield County and western Connecticut work on dedicated route days, so Danbury accounts get a consistent schedule. Every visit is covered by our 48 hour re-clean guarantee.

Areas we cover in Danbury include:

  • Office and corporate parks near I-84
  • Newtown Road and Federal Road retail
  • Medical buildings near the hospital
  • Downtown Main Street storefronts and offices

Every Danbury property gets a free site walk before we quote anything, since a storefront on Newtown Road and a multi story office building are not priced the same way. You will have a written quote within 48 hours of that walk.
