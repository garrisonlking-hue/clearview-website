Questions

Good Questions, Straight Answers.

Will the clips damage my gutters or shingles?

No. We don't use nails or staples. Our clips grip the shingle, gutter, or trim and hold through wind, ice, and CT winters without damaging the roof.

What if a bulb or strand goes out during the season?

Bulb fixes are free for the entire season. If anything goes wrong, we come back and fix it.

Do I keep the lights after the first year?

No. We own the bulbs, keep them stored, and you lease the display each season. Book next year's install and you get 15% off, because your design is already done.

How long does an install take?

The first install takes a lot longer than the ones after it, because every strand is custom cut to your roofline.

What if it rains on install day?

We change the day. We won't install in the rain.

Do you take the lights down and store them?

Yes. Every package includes takedown and labeled seasonal storage at our facility, ready for next November.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $1M in general liability coverage through Hiscox. Every install follows the same safety standards as our window work.

How do I book, and when is the rest due?

Request a free quote and we'll follow up with a detailed plan. A 50% deposit is due at booking, and the remaining balance is due after the job is complete.
