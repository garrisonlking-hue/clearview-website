Our Method

Purified Water, No Chemicals, No Streaks

We clean with a purified water fed pole system, using reverse osmosis and deionized water. Pure water carries no minerals, so it dries completely spot free with no soap or chemical residue left behind on the glass.

We clean low rise and mid rise buildings up to 40 feet, about 4 stories from the ground with our purified water fed pole system, and can extend higher for larger jobs. No lifts, no scaffolding, no crews trampling your landscaping.

The same system lets us reach upper floor windows safely from the ground, without ladders propped against your building or lifts blocking your parking lot.
